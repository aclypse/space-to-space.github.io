What is the project idea?

Space is an educational organization for the adaptation and support of gifted and neurodivergent children.
The concept is based on a metaphor:
for many children, the world feels like a different planet — loud, fast, and overwhelming.

Children are represented as gentle alien characters, not as “different”, but as explorers learning how this planet works.

The visual system creates a calm, clear environment that helps children feel safe and oriented.
Design elements (characters, navigation, pictograms, texts) support adaptation, not entertainment.

Space connects the child’s inner world with the outer world — step by step.
